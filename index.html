03 / A BIT ABOUT ME

Code the system.
Consider the player.

I'm Dalton, a Computer Science in Interactive Media and Game Design student at DigiPen Institute of Technology Singapore / SIT.

I'm interested in where implementation meets experience: gameplay scripting, responsive feedback, audio and the choices that make a level work.

Before studying game development, I served in the RSAF, performing pre- and post-flight inspections on F-15 aircraft and weapon-loading duties.

01 / DESIGN

Start with the player.

I approach design by thinking about what the player should understand, feel and want to do next. Pacing curves and flow maps help me shape ideas into spaces and interactions, balancing clear direction with room to explore.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Bring the ideas to life.

I use C# and C++ across my projects, alongside tools such as Unity, Godot and FMOD, to turn design ideas into working systems. My contributions span gameplay scripting, audio, animation integration and user interfaces, connecting the details that make a project feel responsive and cohesive.
